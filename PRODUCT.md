# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thai-speaking logistics staff who manage orders and review delivery reports within their permitted branches.

## Product Purpose

Support bill-linked delivery workflows and help staff inspect order totals, delivery status, locations, customers, and staff activity through shared analytics filters.

## Brand Personality

Clear, orderly, practical. Preserve the existing red identity and Sarabun typography.

## Anti-references

Avoid the previous analytics filter layout: related fields split across rows, mismatched controls, and a search button mixed into the field grid.

## Design Principles

- Keep every analytics filter visible, grouped by time, location, and people/delivery.
- Place related controls together and keep the primary action in its own predictable position.
- Allow multiple bill-opening employees to be selected and removed without crowding other controls.
- Preserve existing data behavior and branch permissions when adjusting presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Thai labels with explicit control associations, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, and layouts that work on desktop and mobile. Treat these as implementation goals, not a claim of certified conformance.
